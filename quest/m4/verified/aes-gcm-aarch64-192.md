# [XL] aarch64's eight-way AES-192-GCM kernels are proven

## Goal

aws-lc's `aesv8_gcm_8x_enc_192` and `aesv8_gcm_8x_dec_192`, which rind runs for AES-192-GCM on aarch64 cores with the SHA3 instructions (Apple, Graviton 3 and later, Neoverse N2 and V), are proven in Lean on VG's aarch64 model, for the whole blocks rind hands them, in place, so AES-192-GCM earns the ✓ on those cores.

## Plan

Found by the 2026-11-09 audit: [kixel-ai/rind#460](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/460) landed these kernels hours after the 2026-11-07 audit planned AES-192 as AES-CTR and GHASH in turn, so no quest covered them.

- Runs (`low/aarch64/aes_gcm.rs:366-374`, `:388-418`): `low/aarch64/aws_lc/aesv8_gcm_8x_{enc,dec}_192.*.s`, 1,393 and 1,383 lines from aws-lc's `aesv8-gcm-armv8-unroll8.pl`, eight blocks at a time on `eor3`, called past the short pass's lengths with a whole number of blocks, the same input and output pointer, the running GHASH, the counter block, 13 round keys, and the 12-entry H table. Cores without SHA3 run AES-CTR and GHASH in turn, which `Rind/Aarch64/AesGcmComposed.lean` proves. aws-lc's `d313cb9c`, which [kixel-ai/rind#1183](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1183) pins, changes only these files' header line, so what is proven holds at both pins.
- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#1189](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1189), on VG's model with rind's extension, each resting on Lean's three axioms alone:
  - `RindIsa` gained LDP and STP of `q` registers, LDR of a `d` register, and CMP of two registers.
  - `RindIsa/AArch64/AesGcm8x192.lean` transcribes both main loops' bodies, 306 instructions each. `Proven.lean`'s `provenBlocks` lists them, so `admin/prove lean` fails unless each function's `.text` in rind's build holds the body's words in a row, and `admin/prove vg` cosimulates their instructions.
  - `Rind/Aarch64/AesGcm8x192Ops.lean` proves what the loops' instructions compute in rind's AES-GCM vocabulary: AESE and AESMC are Vale's rounds (`aesRounds192_eq`, twelve of them Vale's AES-192), PMULL is `clmul`, REV64 is SP 800-38D's byte order with the halves swapped, REV32 and ADD step the counter (`add_ctr`), both loops' reductions are `reduceModel`'s (`code_reduce`, `code_reduce'`), and SP 800-38D's GHASH of `x :: xs` is `reduceAll` of the blocks by the table's powers (`nistGhash_reduceAll`).
  - `Rind/Aarch64/AesGcm8x192Loop.lean` proves one iteration of each main loop (`encLoop_ok`, `decLoop_ok`): from `LoopPre` (lane round keys at `x11`, `htable8` of the twisted key at `x6`, the reduction constant at `x10`, counter blocks `c` to `c + 5` in `v0`–`v4` and `v30`, the step in `v31`, eight readable blocks at `x0` and writable ones at `x2`), it writes the blocks XORed with AES-192 of counters `c` to `c + 7` to `x2`, hashes the eight blocks it started with into `v19` by `nistGhash`, leaves the ciphertext in `v8`–`v15` and counters `c + 8` to `c + 13`, steps `x0` and `x2` by 128, sets the flags as `cmp x0, x5`, and changes nothing else.
- Left, in order:
  - The whole functions as programs. aws-lc's tails enter a chain of single-block steps from eight `b.gt` sites, and its zero-length `cbz` jumps past the `ret` that ends the body to a second one, which VG's structured `Code` cannot lay out. The route waits on the decision below. On route (a), the copy keeps aws-lc's instructions and duplicates the tail chain per entry, the way the ChaCha20-Poly1305 copy duplicates its shared tails, and stays only if it measures within noise of aws-lc's on an M-series core and a Neoverse N2 (a throwaway A/B harness that calls both directly, as [kixel-ai/rind#1118](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1118) did); the main loop bodies stay as transcribed, so the iteration lemmas carry over unchanged, and `provenBlocks` gives way to the programs in `proven`.
  - What the programs use beyond the model now: MOVI of `#0` and of a byte to 8 and 16 lanes, EOR of 64-bit vectors, MVN of `xzr`, STP of a general register and `xzr` through `sp`, LSRV, ADD of a register shifted right, and CSEL with `xzr`. Each needs its semantics, encoding, `Cosim/Rind.lean` case, and `Rind/Aarch64/Taint.lean` step and soundness case, as do the five this PR added.
  - Functional correctness of each program against `GcmKernelOut`, for a nonzero whole number of blocks below `2^57`, in place, with `KernelBuffersLow`: the prologue (the frame of `d8`–`d15` and the reduction constant at `sp + 64`, the first eight counter blocks and their AES, the first eight blocks when there are more than eight), the main loop composed from the iteration lemmas (the stores at `x2` miss the keys, the table, the constant, and the blocks still to load, so `LoopPre` carries over; `loop_ok` needs a fixed trace, so either a variant of it with the trace existential or the trace as a function of the pointers), the prepretail (the last full group's GHASH beside the last keystream), the tail (one to eight blocks, each by its power of H, with the mask all ones for whole blocks, then the reduction), and the epilogue (`Xi` back in SP 800-38D's order, the counter, and the frame restored).
  - Constant time: `check` accepts both programs (`decide +kernel`), with public `x0`–`x6`, `sp`, and the frame.
  - Glue: s2n-bignum has no AES-192 cipher, so add `aes192Cipher` beside `aes128Cipher` with its `aes192Cipher_eq` to Vale's (`aesRounds192_eq` gives the rounds); then one axiom per routine in `Rind/Aarch64/`, stated over `Rind/S2nGcm.lean`'s vocabulary as the s2n-bignum kernels' are in `Rind/Aarch64/S2nBignum.lean`, recorded in `Axioms.lean`, and the two match arms join `stitched_spec` in `Rind/Aarch64/AesGcmLong.lean`, which then drops its `alg ≠ .AES_192` for tables with `eight_way`.
- s2n-bignum has no AES-192 kernel and no PR for one (searched 2026-11-09). Its `aes{128,256}_gcm_{enc,dec}_alt` are whole-block forms of the same aws-lc eight-way code, proven against `common/gcm.ml` in shared files (`arm/proofs/utils/aes_gcm_enc_alt_proof.ml`, `aes_gcm_enc_alt_common.ml`, `aes_gcm_dec_alt_common.ml`), and show the invariants of the prologue, prepretail, and tail.
- Traffic is low: no TLS 1.3 or QUIC cipher suite uses AES-192, so this ranks behind every AES-128 and AES-256 proof.
- Upstream: offering this work to awslabs/s2n-bignum is optional, and there is no standing approval: stop and ask the maintainer before posting there or anywhere outside [the orgs AGENTS.md lists](/AGENTS.md#development) (decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-07 on [kixel-ai/rind#448](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/448)).

## Waiting

- Maintainer: aws-lc's eight-way AES-192-GCM tails enter a chain of single-block steps from eight branches, which VG's structured `Code` cannot lay out, so the whole kernels cannot be programs in rind's model as aws-lc's bytes stand. Which route should the rest of the proof take?
  - (a) rind's structured copy (Recommended) - keep aws-lc's instructions, duplicate the tail chain per entry, and keep the copy only if it measures within noise of aws-lc's on an M-series core and a Neoverse N2, as the maintainer chose for ChaCha20-Poly1305 on kixel-ai/rind#1077
  - (b) unstructured-code semantics in RindIsa - keep aws-lc's bytes as they are, at the cost of a new trusted control-flow model and a layout and taint checker for it
