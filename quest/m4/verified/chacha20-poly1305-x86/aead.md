# [XL] aws-lc's ChaCha20-Poly1305 seal and open are proven on x86_64

## Goal

`OnePass` in `proofs/Rind/X86_64/ChaCha20Poly1305.lean` is discharged, so ChaCha20-Poly1305 and XChaCha20-Poly1305 earn the ✓ on x86_64 CPUs with SSE4.1 wherever `HaveIfmaForVgChaCha20Poly1305`'s check for the message's length fails: without AVX-512 IFMA, and on Zen 4 sealing under 800 bytes and opening under 512.

## Plan

- Prove both halves (AVX2 and SSE4.1) of aws-lc's `chacha20_poly1305_{seal,open}` on rind's object code in Lean, on VG's x86_64 model, directly against `Rind/Spec.lean`'s ChaCha20 and Poly1305, with their leakage contracts, as [kixel-ai/rind#912](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/912) proved aarch64 SHA-256 (its body measures that route against HOL Light): model what VG's lacks in `proofs/verified-garbage/RindIsa/`, and list the programs in `RindIsa/Proven.lean`, so `admin/prove lean` checks their bytes, the jumps and padding that lay out their structure included, and `admin/prove vg` cosimulates each instruction and branch condition of their `Code`.
- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#1119](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1119): `poly_hash_ad_internal`, the AAD hash seal and open call, is rind's structured copy (`rind/src/low/x86_64/poly_hash_ad.s`, `RindIsa/X86_64/PolyHashAd.lean`, listed in `Proven.lean`; `admin/import-aws-lc` lists it as `owned` and writes no copy of aws-lc's), and `Rind/X86_64/PolyHashAdProof.lean` proves `poly_hash_ad_internal_correct`: from `rcx` and `r8` the AAD and `rbp` a clamped `r`, the accumulator in `(r10, r11, r12)` below `5 · 2^128` and RFC 8439's over the zero-padded AAD modulo `p`, with a trace of the AAD's address and length and `r`'s address only. Seal's and open's proofs take it as the contract of their `call`. It measured within noise of aws-lc's on CodSpeed's chacha20poly1305 and xchacha20poly1305 rows, which hash 32 and 13 bytes of AAD under valgrind, whose CPU has AVX2 but no AVX-512, so they run aws-lc's one pass. `Rind/Poly1305Limbs.lean` holds the arch-neutral Poly1305 facts (`padBlocks`, the partial reduction `reduce`, memory as bytes) for these proofs and the aarch64 child's; `Frame` and `loop_ok` moved to `Rind/X86_64/Asm.lean` for every x86_64 routine.
- Route: as [the aarch64 child](/quest/m4/verified/chacha20-poly1305-aarch64/aead.md) decided (maintainer, 2026-11-26), rind's own structured copies in VG's `Code`, each shared tail duplicated into the paths that reach it and the `extra_in` paths dropped, taken only if they measure within noise of aws-lc's, and listed in ALGORITHMS.md as a deliberate change; #1119 took it for `poly_hash_ad_internal`. `layout` now lowers an `if` with an empty branch to one conditional jump and picks short or near jumps as LLVM relaxes them, so a copy's text assembles to `function`'s bytes.
- Tooling: seal and open are 8,138 instructions, too many to transcribe by hand as #1119 did `poly_hash_ad_internal`'s 146. Translate aws-lc's AT&T text, which `admin/import-aws-lc` already reads, to `Code`, duplicating the tails, and print the copy's `.s` from it, with relocations against `chacha20_consts` and `and_masks` and the `call` to `poly_hash_ad_internal`, so `admin/prove lean` checks the copy as it checks #1119's.
- Model: of the 68 mnemonics seal and open use in rind's release build (llvm-objdump), VG's model and rind's extension lack the conditions of `jbe`, `jg`, `jge`, `jl`, and `cmovg`, and `inc`, `pinsrb`, `pextrb`, and `pextrq`; add them to `RindIsa/X86_64.lean`, which `admin/prove vg` cosimulates.
- Restate them in `Rind/X86_64/` as axioms about `one_pass` that give `OnePass`, record them in `Axioms.lean`, and add the `verified.toml` entries.
- Seal and open are 8,138 instructions; s2n-bignum's proofs of its aarch64 AES-GCM routines run 11,000 to 14,000 lines, a guide to the scale. #1119's proof of `poly_hash_ad_internal`, whose block arithmetic seal and open repeat, runs about 1,000 lines.
- pyca/verified-garbage (VG) measured slower on 2026-11-13 (ChaCha20-Poly1305 on x86_64 up to 1.14x rind's time with AVX-512 and 1.68x without AVX2), so rind keeps this code ([the m2 VG epic](/quest/m2/verified-garbage/README.md)). `fedora`'s Zen 5 at VG `0aa40d6c` reopened AVX-512, and [kixel-ai/rind#953](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/953) moved CPUs with AVX-512 IFMA to VG at every length, and [kixel-ai/rind#1108](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1108) kept Zen 4 on aws-lc's below 800 bytes sealing and 512 opening, so this quest covers the CPUs and lengths that fail that check, where `RunsSeal` and `RunsOpen` take `OnePass`; the rest rests on VG's theorems, which `proofs/Rind/X86_64/VgChaCha20Poly1305.lean` imports.
