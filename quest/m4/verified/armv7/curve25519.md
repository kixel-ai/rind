# [XL] armv7's Curve25519 is proven

## Goal

X25519 and Ed25519 earn the ✓ on armv7.

## Plan

- Runs: curve25519-dalek's serial 32-bit backend for X25519 key generation's base multiplication and all of Ed25519, under rind's canonical-y and small-order checks; with NEON, BoringSSL's `neon2` X25519 assembly (from SUPERCOP) for the shared secret. HACL*'s X25519 was 1.9x slower than `neon2` and its Ed25519 3.5x slower to sign, so switching fails the speed rule.
- Already proven: nothing. [Beneficial AI Foundation's Lean proofs of curve25519-dalek](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/curve25519-dalek-lean-verify/blob/52cad87d054943c96c984d99d9ea9e5d043b0d65/status.csv), through Aeneas, cover its serial 64-bit backend only.
- Route: Aeneas to Lean for dalek's 32-bit field arithmetic, reusing those proofs' point and scalar layers and specs where they are backend-generic, and for rind's glue against `Rind/Ed25519.lean`'s spec; `neon2` on [the AArch32 model](/quest/m4/verified/armv7/model.md).

## Required

- [armv7 runs the Rust proofs](/quest/m4/verified/armv7/prove.md) - the armv7 translation
- [An AArch32 instruction model checks armv7's assembly](/quest/m4/verified/armv7/model.md) - the model `neon2`'s proof runs on
