# [XL] aws-lc's ChaCha20-Poly1305 seal and open are proven on aarch64

## Goal

`AwsLc` in `proofs/Rind/Aarch64/ChaCha20Poly1305.lean` is discharged, so ChaCha20-Poly1305 and XChaCha20-Poly1305 earn the ✓ at every length on aarch64 CPUs without SVE2, as they do with it.

## Plan

- Runs: rind's structured copy of aws-lc's NEON `chacha20_poly1305_{seal,open}` (`low/aarch64/chacha20poly1305/`), which interleaves NEON ChaCha20 with scalar Poly1305, on CPUs without SVE2 at the lengths `seal_runs_vg` and `open_runs_vg` leave to it: sealing up to 576 bytes and from 769 to 896, and opening up to 128, from 257 to 448, and from 896 to 960.
- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#1118](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1118), on route (a), which the maintainer chose on [kixel-ai/rind#1077](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1077):
  - The copy keeps aws-lc's instructions, inlines `poly_hash_ad_internal`, drops `extra_in`, and lays each routine out as VG's `Code`. It measured within noise of aws-lc's on an Apple M5 Pro and a Neoverse N2, and ALGORITHMS.md lists it as a deliberate change.
  - `RindIsa` gained the instructions it uses, B.cond, and `ite`/`loop` layout.
  - `RindIsa/AArch64/ChaCha20Poly1305.lean` holds `sealProg` and `openProg`, which `admin/prove lean` checks byte for byte and `admin/prove vg` cosimulates.
  - `Rind/Aarch64/ChaCha20Poly1305Asm.lean` proves both programs constant-time for public `x0`–`x5`, `sp`, and the constants' address, through the kernel-checked taint checker in `Rind/Aarch64/Taint.lean`.
- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#1147](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1147):
  - `Rind/Aarch64/Asm.lean` runs straight-line blocks of rind's AArch64 model symbolically, with frames and a loop lemma, as `Rind/X86_64/Asm.lean` does for x86_64.
  - `Rind/Aarch64/ChaCha20Poly1305Poly.lean` proves the Poly1305 block step at the level of numbers (`limbs_mulOut`, `limbs_addT`, `acc_step`: 3 limbs times a clamped 2-limb `r`, partially reduced mod 2^130 − 5) and runs `polyAdd` and `polyMul` on the model. `limbs` and `reduce_limbs` moved to `Rind/Poly1305Limbs.lean`, which the x86_64 proofs import.
  - `Rind/Aarch64/ChaCha20Poly1305Ad.lean` proves the inlined AAD hash, `adBlocks` then `adTail`, all four copies (`ad_ok`), with traces of the AAD's address and length alone.
  - `Rind/Aarch64/ChaCha20Poly1305Named.lean` restates both programs in named blocks, `sealNamed` and `openNamed`, equal to `sealProg` and `openProg` by `rfl`: all 31 copies of the multiply-and-reduce (`polyMul`, inside `polyBlock` or `polyAdd ++ polyMul`), the AAD hash, and the double round over one, two, or three blocks (`doubleRound1` to `doubleRound3`).
- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#1191](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1191), ChaCha20's rounds and the blocks around some of them, in two new files:
  - `ChaCha20Poly1305Named.lean` names `roundA` and `roundB`, the halves of the five-block double round that seal's first rounds run back to back and the main loops run with `polyBlock` between; `setup128` and `final128`, the short paths' blocks before and after their rounds, the same in seal and open; and `finalInit`, the block after seal's first rounds. `sealProg_eq` and `openProg_eq` still hold by `rfl`.
  - `Rind/Aarch64/ChaCha20Poly1305Rounds.lean` treats each register as four 32-bit lanes (`ofVWords`) and each instruction of the rounds as a lane-wise operation on them, so `simp` runs a block symbolically and matches every register against RFC 8439's `innerBlock` in seconds: `doubleRound1_ok` to `doubleRound3_ok`, block `i`'s rows in `v<i>`, `v<5+i>`, `v<10+i>`, `v<15+i>` (`Rows`); `roundA_ok`, `roundB_ok`, and `round5_ok`, four blocks in vertical lanes (`Vert`, word `i` of block `l` in lane `l` of `v<5 (i / 4) + i % 4>`) and a fifth in `v4`, `v9`, `v14`, `v19`; and the loops of ten double rounds on `x6`, `rounds1_ok`, `rounds3_ok`, and `rounds5_ok`.
  - `Rind/Aarch64/ChaCha20Poly1305Block.lean` states the block function as `keyBlock` (`block_eq`), each block's state as `bump` of the rows in `v24` and `v28` to `v30`, and proves `setup128_ok` and `final128_ok` (the short paths' key stream for the next two counters, and Poly1305's clamped `r` in `x16` and `x17` and `s` in `v27`), and `finalInit_ok` (seal's first four key-stream blocks turned from vertical lanes to rows, and Poly1305's key from the fifth).
- Left: functional correctness of `sealNamed` and `openNamed` in the model against `Rind/Spec.lean`'s RFC 8439, as `Rind/Aarch64/Sha256HwAsm.lean` proves SHA-256's routine: from a `Pre` (lengths, a 16-byte-aligned stack with 64 writable bytes below it, readable input, AAD, and constants, writable output and data block, input equal to or disjoint from output; `seal_aws_lc` and `open_aws_lc` call them in place, so equal alone gives `AwsLc`), the output holds `chacha20.encrypt key 1 nonce msg` and the data block's first 16 bytes the tag, d8–d15, `sp`, and `x30` are kept, and some state meets `Pre`. Traces can stay existential (`∃ t s', Exec …`), since `ChaCha20Poly1305Asm.lean` already proves the leakage half.
  - The blocks that load ChaCha20's states from memory, which need the memory facts below: the prologue's `ld1` of the constants and the data block into `v24` to `v30`, and seal's `ld4r` of the vertical lanes before its first rounds; then the remaining blocks around rounds, each as `final128_ok` and `finalInit_ok` are: both main loops' setup and finish (counters from `v25`, the fifth block's counter through `not`, `sub`, and `ext`), open's first block (`doubleRound1` on block 0) and its 64-, 128-, and 192-byte tails, whose hash loops run some of the ten double rounds (`open_tail_128_hash`, `open_tail_64_hash_loop`).
  - The main loops' round nests, which interleave `polyBlock` with `roundA` and `roundB` (`round5_ok` composes the halves; a `polyBlock` between them keeps the vector registers, so its frame carries `Vert` and `Rows` across) on two counters, `x6` and `x7`, that go negative, so their flags need a signed form of `SubFlags`.
  - Memory: a 16-byte load as four little-endian words (`Mem.read`, `chacha20.le32`), stores, and byte lists (`bytesAt`) against `keyBlock`'s serialization; then the key-stream XOR and stores (`ld1`, `eor`, `st1`), the ciphertext hash loops, which run `polyBlock` or `umovs ++ polyAdd ++ polyMul` (`hash_ok`) over the ciphertext as `ad_ok` does over the AAD, the partial-block byte loops (`ext` by 15 and 1, `ldrb`, `strb`), and the lengths block, final reduction, and tag.
  - Compose the paths: seal's 128-byte, initial, main-loop, and tail paths, open's likewise, each a `seqs` of the blocks above.
  - A throwaway interpreter for `Code` ran both programs in the model against the spec at 22 lengths from 1 to 1000 bytes, every branch path, all matching: the model and the programs agree with RFC 8439 at those lengths.
- Then restate the theorems in `Rind/Aarch64/` as axioms about `seal_aws_lc` and `open_aws_lc` that give `AwsLc`, as `Rind/Aarch64/Sha256Hw.lean` restates SHA-256's, and record them in `Axioms.lean`.
