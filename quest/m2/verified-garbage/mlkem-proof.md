## Goal

ML-KEM-768 earns the ✓ on the tiers that run pyca/verified-garbage's (VG) routines: its encapsulation key check and encapsulation on every x86_64 CPU and on aarch64 with the SHA-3 instructions, and its key generation and decapsulation on x86_64 with AVX-512 (`low/<arch>/mlkem768_vg.rs`, vendored by `admin/import-vg`).

## Plan

- Done: `proofs/Rind/<Arch>/VgMlKem.lean` restates VG's contract for each routine as one axiom about rind's opaque wrapper in `low::<arch>::mlkem768_vg`, over VG's FIPS 203 spec (`Spec/MlKem.lean`, vendored by `admin/import-vg-models`).
