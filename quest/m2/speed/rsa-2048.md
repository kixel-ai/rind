# [M] RSA-2048 signing as fast as aws-lc-rs

## Goal

RSA-2048 signing (and 3072 and 4096) is as fast as the fastest of aws-lc-rs
and ring on every x86_64 tier and on aarch64, or within the ~10% `AGENTS.md`
allows for verified code, on every host the
[benchmark report](/quest/m1/benchmarks/README.md) measures. JWT's RS256,
the most common JWT algorithm, reaches Bun, pyrind, and llrt through it.

## Plan

Planned with the maintainer on 2026-11-04, for the adoption epic's
consumers.

- The nightly bench puts x86_64 signing at 1.08 of ring's time on Zen 3; an
  i7-8700 (Coffee Lake, ADX) measured 599 µs to ring's 457 and aws-lc-rs's
  476, about 1.3x, in a rough run on a busy host. Confirm on the benchmark
  hosts before changing code.
- [moq-dev/rind#224](https://github.com/moq-dev/rind/pull/224) chose
  s2n-bignum's verified Karatsuba over aws-lc's faster `bn_powerx5` and
  `bn_mul_mont_gather5`, inside the 10% rule. Where a host shows more than
  that, take aws-lc's routines on that path and record the reason, or find
  the speed elsewhere (CRT recombination, blinding, the exponentiation's
  window).
