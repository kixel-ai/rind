# [L] armv7's NEON AES is proven

## Goal

aws-lc's `bsaes-armv7` and `vpaes-armv7` NEON routines, which armv7 runs for AES and AES-GCM without the crypto extension, are proven on the AArch32 model against FIPS 197, and composed in Lean with `Rind/Armv7/GhashNeon.lean`'s NEON GHASH and the Rust proofs of rind's AES-GCM, so those operations earn the ✓ on that tier.

## Plan

Decided 2026-11-18 (maintainer, [kixel-ai/rind#619](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/619#issuecomment-6994276361)): the proof of the NEON AES switch, which [kixel-ai/rind#913](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/913) made, goes under this epic.

- Route: as [armv7's assembly](/quest/m5/verified-armv7/assembly.md) proves the other routines, against the FIPS 197 and SP 800-38D specs the x86_64 and aarch64 quests write, composed with the Rust proofs of rind's AES-GCM and mode glue.
- AES-GCM above `AesGcm` is proven on any `GcmReady` key (`Rind/Armv7/AesGcm.lean`): this tier proves that `AesGcm::new` on a CPU with NEON but not the crypto extension keys `Neon` with `PartsFor` its `Arm`, and extends `RunsAesGcm` and `aes_gcm_new_spec` to it.
- NEON GHASH is done: `Rind/Armv7/GhashNeon.lean` restates [kixel-ai/rind#1088](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1088)'s HOL Light theorems and proves `neon_new_ghashFor`, the `GhashFor` that `PartsFor`'s table field asks. What remains is `NeonKey`'s `encrypt_block` and `ctr32`, then `PartsFor` for `Arm<NeonKey, NeonTable>`.
- `admin/prove`'s `asm_routine` extracts each of `vpaes_armv7.rs`'s `global_asm!` routines by its symbol, as it does GHASH's: one `hol_light_proofs` entry and proof file per routine. It refuses relocations, and `vpaes_encrypt` has two: `bl _vpaes_preheat` and `bl _vpaes_encrypt_core` are `R_ARM_CALL`s to local functions, and the core reads `_vpaes_consts` relative to its PC. So its extraction spans `_vpaes_consts` through `vpaes_encrypt`'s return, 0x320 bytes in the object, with the two same-section calls resolved as the linker resolves them; s2n-bignum's `elf.ml` rejects `SHT_REL` sections, so resolve them in `admin/prove`. The theorem needs `128 divides pc`, since `.align 7` and the round counter's `bic r11, r11, #64` wrap assume the tables sit 128-byte aligned.
- vpaes's math, checked against FIPS 197 for 128-, 192-, and 256-bit keys by a Python model of the routine and of `vpaes_set_encrypt_key`. With T the byte-wise GF(2)-linear `ipt` transform (`T(0x63) = 0x5b`; `opt` is its inverse) and `sr[j]` the ShiftRows-power permutations, the state after i middle rounds is `tbl(T(s_i), sr[(-i) mod 4])` for AES state `s_i`. The stored keys are `K_0 = T(k_0)`, `K_j = perm_{sr[(-j) mod 4]}(T(mix3(k_j)) ^ 0x5b)` for middle rounds 1 to n, where `mix3` is `rot + rot^2 + rot^3` of each column and `rot` the `mc_forward[0]` lookup, and the last `perm_{sr[(-n) mod 4]}(k_{n+1} ^ 0x63)`; offset 240 stores n = bits/32 + 5. Middle round i uses `mc_forward[i mod 4]` and `mc_backward[i mod 4]`, and the output lookup is `sr[(n+1) mod 4]`. Byte by byte, a middle round is `A[bk p] ^ A[f p] ^ 2A[p] ^ A[f f p] ^ 2A[f p]`, with `A = sb1(io, jo) ^ K` and `2A = sb2(io, jo)`. On ARM, io indexes `sb1`'s and `sb2`'s second table and `sbo`'s first, unlike x86's naming. So the proof splits into machine theorems against a vpaes-shaped spec, per-byte lemmas (256 cases each, checked by the model) that, with `y = S(T⁻¹(x)) ^ 0x63` for the byte x the round's inverse tables read, the `sb1` stage returns `T(y)`, the `sb2` stage `T(2·y)` in the AES field, and the `sbo` stage `y`, the keys' `0x5b` and `0x63` restoring the S-box's constant, and permutation algebra relating `mc_*` and `sr` to ShiftRows and MixColumns.
- Symbolic simulation of the middle round (0x210 to 0x274 in the object) with `AARCH32_STEPS_TAC` took about 18 s an instruction, with registers reaching 12K characters by instruction 20, because `AARCH32_SIMD_EXPAND_CONV` unfolds `aarch32_tbl` into a conditional per lane. Keep each variable-index lookup folded as `aarch32_tbl 2 (word C) x`, through a step conversion without `aarch32_tbl` in its expansions or a fold after each step, and abbreviate `A` and `2A` to fresh variables before the `mc` permutations. The table loads need the 44 constant D-register reads from `aligned_bytes_loaded` restated before each step, since the stepper drops `word_add (word (pc + a)) (word 8)` forms; normalize `word_add`, `word_sub`, and the `bic` mask into `word (pc + c)` after each step.
- Bitsliced AES needs a proof that its bit-sliced S-box circuit computes the S-box; vpaes's needs its permutation tables proven to compose to it. Look for an existing proof of either (s2n-bignum, Fiat, Jasmin) before writing one. The fork's `common/fips197.ml` has AES-128 and AES-256 only; rind's schedules include AES-192.
- Lessons from the GHASH proof: a NEON macro's leftover terms in scratch registers make each later step slower, so rename them to fresh variables between macros (its `FORGET_TAC`); name each Q register's initial value before a D-half write; and `BITBLAST_RULE` proves linear identities over 400 variables in seconds once nonlinear pieces are generalized to atoms.
